4 How We Use Information

  • To create and operate your account, and to show your listings to nearby Buyers;
  • To calculate distances and show listings within a Buyer's chosen search radius;
  • To look up nearby points of interest (schools, malls, supermarkets) for a listing, using only the listing's location — never your personal saved locations;
  • To send transactional emails (sign-in links, password resets, one-time passcodes for email-OTP sign-in, seller notifications when a Buyer expresses interest);
  • To automatically screen buyer-authored messages for spam/fraud signals before notifying a Seller, so genuine enquiries aren't lost among low-effort or automated ones. This screening never blocks a message from reaching the Seller — it only adds a label;
  • To detect and remove orphaned files, prevent abuse, and maintain the security of the Service;
  • To detect fraud and abuse, and to comply with legal record-keeping requirements under the IT Rules, 2021 (IP address and access timestamps associated with account creation, sign-in, listing creation, and messaging — see Section 9);
  • To respond to feedback and support requests.

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, given when you register for the Service and agree to this Policy, and, where applicable, on the basis of legitimate uses reasonably expected in the course of providing a location-listing service (for example, showing your listing's location to Buyers, which is the core function you signed up for).

6 Location Data — Special Notice

MapIt distinguishes between two kinds of location data:

  • Listing location (public): the GPS coordinates attached to a published listing are visible to any user Browse the Service.
  • Personal/saved location (private): a home address or other pin saved to your profile for search convenience is used only to power your own search radius and is never shown to other users, and never sold or shared with advertisers.

Because listing locations are precise and public by design, do not attach your home address to a listing unless you are comfortable with that exact point being publicly visible.
