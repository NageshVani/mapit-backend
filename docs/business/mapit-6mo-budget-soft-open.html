Infrastructure and tools only — no incorporation, legal, or founder compensation. Sized for a bootstrapped runway, not a raise.

Assumptions

Signup ramp: 50 → 100 → 200 → 300 → 400 → 500 across months 1–6, ~1,550 signups total — a soft-open curve, not a marketing push.
  • OTP volume: 1.3 SMS attempts per signup (covers resends) ≈ 2,015 OTP messages over 6 months.
  • SMS provider: an India-registered aggregator (MSG91-class — no monthly fee, prepaid wallet, ₹0.15–0.25/OTP), not Twilio. Twilio's India route runs ₹9–14 per OTP after forex and per-verification fees — 60–80× the local rate for the identical message.
  • DLT registration (TRAI Principal Entity) is a carrier-level requirement, not provider-specific — it's paid once regardless of which aggregator is chosen.

How to proceed from here

↑ If it takes off

Trigger: signups tracking meaningfully above the 500/month curve, or Supabase/Vercel usage alerts firing before month-end
  1. Re-negotiate SMS rate.Most India aggregators drop from ~₹0.20 to ~₹0.12–0.15/OTP past 10k messages/month — ask before you're forced to.
  2. Watch Supabase egress and connections first, not storage.Photo uploads and realtime chat connections hit limits before raw row count does — set a usage alert, don't wait for an outage.
  3. Set a Vercel spend cap.Pro overage billing is uncapped by default — add a budget alert so a traffic spike is a notification, not a surprise invoice.
  4. Throttle with an allowlist, not a rewrite.If infra can't keep pace, a temporary invite/waitlist gate buys weeks without touching code.
  5. Treat rapid growth as the trigger to revisit the incorporation/CA conversation.That's a separate budget line (memo §E) — this pass deliberately excluded it, but growth is exactly the signal that reopens it.

↓ If it stalls

Trigger: signups tracking well under the ramp curve by month 3, or engagement (repeat logins, listings posted) not following signups
  1. Know the real fixed floor.Only the domain (~₹1,250/yr) is unavoidable. Vercel, Supabase, and SMS all step back to ₹0 on their own tiers — nothing here is a locked-in contract.
  2. Downgrade Supabase before it auto-pauses.Free-tier projects pause after a week of inactivity — plan the downgrade deliberately so you're not recovering from an unplanned pause mid-debug.
  3. SMS wallet already fails safe.Prepaid, no monthly commitment — stop topping up and spend stops with it. Nothing to actively cancel.
  4. Let DLT lapse, don't renew.It's an annual fee tied to actually sending SMS — if OTP login isn't running, there's nothing to keep registered.
  5. Set one hard checkpoint, not a slow drift.Pick a single date (e.g., end of month 3) to compare real signups against this ramp assumption and decide — continue, pause, or shut down — rather than letting the decision happen by default.
