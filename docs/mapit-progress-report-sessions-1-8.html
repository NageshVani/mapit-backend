What's next

Sessions 7 and 8 are code-complete and just need Nagesh's UAT sign-off. Once that's done, the plan is to work through everything in Session 9 (and any Session 10 items) that does not require a paid subscription — Vercel Pro, Supabase Pro, and Resend's paid tier are all deliberately being held until the last stretch before go-live, so recurring costs stay at $0 for as long as possible.

  • Held for last: Vercel Pro ($20/mo), Supabase Pro ($25/mo), Resend Starter ($20/mo) — bundled into one upgrade step immediately before public launch.
  • Unlocked by that upgrade (parked until then): the listing-expiry reminder cron, database connection pooling, Storage CDN, backup/PITR verification.
  • Final gate before going live: a 9-point security and readiness checklist (CORS, CSP, RLS, error monitoring, legal pages, email verification, OAuth redirects, no leaked service keys), followed by a 48-hour soft-open to a small outside group.
