🎬 Suggested Demo Flow (~8–10 min)

A live walkthrough order that shows the most visually convincing improvements first, then the "trust" story underneath.
Open mapit.co.in on desktop. Pan the map anywhere in Bangalore with "🗺 Explore Area" toggled on — watch the blue radius circle follow you and listings auto-refresh. Switch back to "📍 My Locations" to show it defaults back to the user's saved spot.
Open any listing near a school or mall — point out the new "📍 Nearby" section showing distance to the nearest school/mall/supermarket. This is fully automatic, no seller input needed.
Post 2 test listings at the same address — show the map collapsing them into one pin with a count badge, tap it to open the mini-list. (Great for apartment complexes / bazaars.)
Start posting a new listing — show the location picker: type an area name to pan the map, tap to drop a pin, or paste GPS coordinates from a Google Maps long-press. All three converge on the same pin.
Tap "I'm Interested" on a listing as a buyer, type a real note, send it — then show the seller's inbox: an actual email arrived with the buyer's own words (not a canned message).
Switch to a phone (or narrow the browser) — show the Map/List toggle and tap a pin to demonstrate the preview-then-open tap behavior built specifically for touch scanning.
Close with the trust story: mention the RLS security audit found and closed two real data leaks (other users' phone numbers/home addresses were readable before the fix) — this is the kind of hardening that happens before a wider launch, not after.

🔍 Search & Discovery

Making it faster to find the right listing, wherever the buyer is looking.

🗺 Explore Area mode LIVE

A new toggle lets buyers pan the map to any part of the city and search from there — not just their saved home location. Listings auto-reload as they stop panning.

Why it matters: buyers relocating or scouting a new neighborhood aren't stuck searching only around home.

🔎 Keyword search, restored to the header LIVE

Type any word and instantly filter listings by title/description, respecting whatever area is currently in view on the map.

Why it matters: the fastest path to "I know exactly what I want" searches.

📍 Nearby POIs on every listing LIVE

Every new listing automatically shows the nearest named school, mall, and supermarket with distance — fully automatic, no seller data entry.

Why it matters: a real-estate-style amenity signal that costs nothing extra to build (free OpenStreetMap data).

📌 Stacked pins for same-address listings LIVE

Apartment complexes and bazaars with multiple listings at the identical address now show one badged pin with a scrollable mini-list, instead of overlapping markers.

Why it matters: the map stays readable exactly in the dense areas where MapIt's core promise (exact GPS) matters most.

🛡 Trust & Safety

Protecting user data and giving sellers confidence that real buyers are reaching out.

🔐 Full RLS security audit LIVE

A direct database-level audit found and fixed two real gaps: any logged-in user could previously read every other user's phone number and home address, and an unauthenticated visitor could read the (now-retired) invite-codes table.

Why it matters: the kind of finding that matters a lot more before a public launch than after one.

📧 Seller email on "I'm Interested" LIVE

Sellers now get an email notification the moment a buyer expresses interest — and the buyer types a real note instead of a fixed canned message, so the email shows genuine buyer intent.

Why it matters: faster seller response times, which is the single biggest lever on marketplace liquidity.

🤖 AI spam/lead screening PENDING YOUR SIGN-OFF

Built and tested — every buyer note gets silently classified as genuine or spam-like using Claude, flagging low-quality contacts for the seller without ever blocking a real message. Fails safe: if the check can't run, the message still always goes through.

Why it matters: the only thing blocking this from going live is your go-ahead on the small recurring API cost (Rule 11 budget discipline).

🔑 Stronger password rules LIVE

New accounts now require 8+ characters, one uppercase letter, and one special character — with live feedback as the user types.

Why it matters: basic account-takeover hygiene before scaling up user count.

✍️ Seller Experience

Making it faster and less confusing to post a listing.

📍 Tap-to-pin location picker LIVE

Posting a listing now uses the same polished flow as registration: search an area by name, tap the map to drop a pin, or paste exact GPS coordinates from a Google Maps long-press — all three converge on the same result.

Why it matters: this directly serves MapIt's core promise — exact GPS on every listing, made as easy as possible to get right.

🗂 Roomier 2-column post form LIVE

The "Post a Listing" form is now a 2-column layout on desktop/tablet — significantly less scrolling to fill in category, details, location, and photos.

Why it matters: shorter forms measurably reduce listing-abandonment.

🙅 No more duplicate signup fields LIVE

New accounts used to be asked for Full Name and Nickname twice in one signup flow. Now asked once.

Why it matters: a small but real friction point removed from the very first thing a new user does.

📱 Mobile Experience

Most of MapIt's real-world usage happens on a phone — these were built and tested on-device.

☰ Map/List view toggle LIVE

On phone-sized screens, a floating toggle switches between full-screen map and full-screen list — and remembers the user's last choice.

Why it matters: more screen real estate for whichever mode the user actually wants at that moment.

👆 Tap-to-preview before opening LIVE

On touch devices, the first tap on a pin shows a quick preview card; a second tap (or an explicit button) opens the full listing — avoids accidentally opening the wrong listing while scanning a busy map.

Why it matters: built specifically in response to real tester feedback on how thumbs actually behave on a map full of pins.

❓ Lightweight Help modal LIVE

A quick in-app guide (Browse, Post, Save, Contact a seller, Delete a listing, Change your location) reachable from the profile menu.

Why it matters: self-serve answers for first-time users, without needing a full support/FAQ system yet.

⚙️ Platform Health & Cost Control

Behind-the-scenes work that keeps the platform cheap to run and easy to operate as it grows.

🧹 Orphaned photo cleanup LIVE

Deleting a listing now also removes its photo files from storage (previously only the listing record was deleted, silently leaving photo files behind forever). A one-time cleanup already recovered 3 leaked files in production.

Why it matters: storage costs scale with listing volume — this keeps that growth honest.

⭐ Feedback form with star ratings LIVE

User feedback (suggestions, bugs, complaints, praise) now includes an optional 1–5 star rating, shown next to each entry in the admin feedback list for faster triage.

Why it matters: quicker to spot the feedback that actually needs attention first.

✉️ Branded transactional emails LIVE

Sign-in and password-reset emails now carry MapIt's branding and colors instead of the generic default template.

Why it matters: every email touchpoint is a brand impression — this one used to look like a generic dev tool.

🔒 CORS & error-handling hardening LIVE

Tightened which domains can call the API, and fixed two lower-severity bugs found during testing (wrong HTTP status on a rejected request, and a debug stack trace that could leak in certain error responses).

Why it matters: standard due-diligence hygiene, the kind that gets asked about in any serious platform review.
