Field note

"Indian alternative" doesn't automatically mean cheaper or better. We ran this exact test on the map-tile layer: Mappls markets itself as ~30% more economical, but publishes no self-serve pricing — quotes require a sales call — and its terms mandate a visible logo on every plan, including paid ones. Mapbox's pricing is public and its free tier already covers MapIt's launch traffic, so that's what shipped.

If cost and India-first billing genuinely matter from day one, look specifically for transparent, self-serve, GST-inclusive pricing — Zoho's stack and MSG91 pass that test. A local brand name on its own doesn't.
