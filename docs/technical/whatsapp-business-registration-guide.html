WhatsApp Business Registration Guide

MapIt — WhatsApp Business Platform setup for Session 9C phone-OTP verification

Written 2026-09-29 BSP: MSG91 SIM owner: Arun Entity: Nakshe Ventures (PAN in hand)
Who does what: Arun obtains the new Indian SIM (Airtel or Jio) and carries out the registration steps below. This document is written so it can be followed start-to-finish without needing to re-derive any of the reasoning behind each step.

1. The overall plan — 250/day now, 1,000/day soon after

MapIt will launch its Bangalore soft-open on WhatsApp's unverified tier (250 OTP conversations per 24 hours) — plenty for an initial soft-open. Separately, Meta Business Verification (which requires legal business documents — Nakshe Ventures' PAN, already in hand) raises that ceiling to 1,000/day once approved.

Key optimization: don't wait until after launch to start Business Verification. Submit it immediately after the WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) is created (Step 6 below), in parallel with going live at 250/day. Verification review (a few days to ~2 weeks) runs independently and doesn't interrupt OTP sending on the unverified tier. By the time MapIt is actually scaling toward the 250/day ceiling, verification will likely have already cleared and the tier raises itself automatically — no manual step needed at "after launch."

2. Getting the SIM — provider & plan type

Owner: Arun. Provider: Airtel or Jio (either is fine — see below). Plan type: postpaid, not prepaid.

ProviderRecommendation
AirtelPreferred — strong, reliable Bangalore coverage; business-postpaid KYC onboarding tends to be smoother for a single "utility" number like this.
JioEqually fine — widest 4G coverage, cheapest plans. Pick this if Arun already has an easier KYC path with Jio.
Vi (Vodafone Idea)Not recommended — less consistent network reliability in Bangalore for something that needs to stay continuously reachable.
Postpaid matters here. Once this number is claimed by the WhatsApp Business Platform (Step 5), it will never again be used as an ordinary phone — no calls, no SMS, no consumer WhatsApp app. A prepaid SIM with no recharge activity risks being deactivated by the telecom after ~90 days of inactivity, and the number can be recycled — which would silently break the WABA. Postpaid with auto-pay avoids this entirely for a trivial monthly cost (~₹200–400).

Functional requirement: the number only needs to be able to receive one SMS or voice-call OTP, during registration (Step 5). After that, it is a WhatsApp-only number.

3. What NOT to do with the new SIM

Do not insert the SIM into a phone and open WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business (the consumer apps) with this number — ever, not even briefly. If either consumer app is activated on this number, Meta enforces a mandatory 24-hour teardown wait before the number can be moved to the Business Platform. Go straight to Step 5 below with this number, skipping the consumer apps entirely.

4. Step-by-step registration procedure

Step 1 — Create the Meta Business Portfolio

  1. Go to business.facebook.com using Nagesh's (or a shared MapIt) Facebook account.
  2. Create a Business Portfolio named "MapIt" — this is the consumer-facing brand name that should appear to users.
  3. Add mapit.co.in as the business website and contactus@mapit.co.in as the business email — both already live, which smooths every later verification step.

Step 2 — Set up MSG91 (the chosen BSP)

  1. Sign up / log in at msg91.com.
  2. Activate the WhatsApp product in the MSG91 dashboard.
  3. MSG91 provides an Embedded Signup flow that drives the entire Meta onboarding from inside their dashboard — no need to touch Meta's raw developer console directly.

Step 3 — Run Embedded Signup (creates the WABA)

  1. Inside MSG91's WhatsApp onboarding, log in with the Meta/Facebook account and select the Business Portfolio from Step 1.
  2. Enter the new phone number. Meta sends a verification code by SMS or voice call to that SIM — enter the code to confirm. (This is the one moment the number needs to function as an actual phone.)
  3. Fill in the WhatsApp Business Profile: display name (MapIt), category (e.g. "Shopping & Retail" or "Classifieds"), a short description, and optionally a business address.
  4. Completing this creates the WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) under the MapIt Business Portfolio, with MSG91 attached as the Tech Provider/BSP.

Step 4 — Display name review

The display name is reviewed separately from the phone number itself — usually 1–3 days. Keep it simple and exactly matching the business identity (MapIt) to avoid rejection or a resubmission cycle.

Step 5 — Submit the OTP template for approval

In WhatsApp Manager (accessible via the MSG91 dashboard or Meta Business Suite), create a new template:

FieldValue
CategoryAuthentication
LanguageEnglish (add more later if needed)
ContentMeta's standard OTP format — verification code + expiry, optionally a "copy code" button

Authentication-category templates are low-customization and typically approve in hours to about a day.

Step 6 — Live at 250/day

Once the display name (Step 4) and the template (Step 5) are both approved, the number is fully live on the unverified tier. MSG91 exposes a send/verify API that gets wired into MapIt's backend — that integration work is Session 9C's build item, tracked separately in CONTEXT.md and running in parallel with, not blocking, this registration process.

Step 7 — Submit Business Verification now, in parallel

  1. In Meta Business Manager → Business Settings → Security Center → Start Verification.
  2. Provide: Nakshe Ventures' PAN, a matching business address proof (bank statement, utility bill, or the firm's registration certificate), and confirm the legal business name matches exactly across every document and the Business Portfolio.
  3. Domain ownership of mapit.co.in may also be requested — either a DNS TXT record (Namecheap access needed) or a confirmed email at that domain (contactus@mapit.co.in already exists and works).
  4. Review typically takes a few days to ~2 weeks. Once approved, the ceiling raises from 250/day to 1,000/day automatically — no further action needed.

5. One naming decision to lock in before Step 1

Register the Business Portfolio/WABA display name as "MapIt" (the consumer-facing brand), but list Nakshe Ventures as the legal entity during Business Verification (Step 7). This is the standard trade-name-vs-legal-entity pattern and avoids Meta's reviewers flagging a business-name mismatch.
