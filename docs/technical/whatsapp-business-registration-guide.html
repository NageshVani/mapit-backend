1. The overall plan — verify first, then OTP goes live

Meta Business Verification (legal business documents for Nakshe Ventures) must be approved before the OTP (Authentication) template can even be created. There is no "go live unverified" path for OTP any more. Once verification clears and the template is approved, MapIt can send OTPs; messaging limits then scale up automatically with volume and quality.

Don't work around it by filing the OTP as a "Utility" or "Marketing" template. Meta re-classifies or rejects templates that carry a verification code, and repeated rejections can hurt the account's quality rating.
Timing matters: MSG91's Titan plan is free only until 31 Oct 2026, then ₹500/month (Section 6). Submit Business Verification as early as possible so the free months aren't used up while waiting. Recent reports put document-based review at about 3–7 business days.

2. Getting the SIM — provider & plan type

Owner: Arun. Provider: Airtel or Jio (either is fine — see below). Plan type: postpaid, not prepaid.

ProviderRecommendation

JioEqually fine — widest 4G coverage, cheapest plans. Pick this if Arun already has an easier KYC path with Jio.
Vi (Vodafone Idea)Not recommended — less consistent network reliability in Bangalore for something that needs to stay continuously reachable.
Postpaid matters here. Once this number is claimed by the WhatsApp Business Platform (Step 3), it will never again be used as an ordinary phone — no calls, no SMS, no consumer WhatsApp app. A prepaid SIM with no recharge activity risks being deactivated by the telecom after ~90 days of inactivity, and the number can be recycled — which would silently break the WABA. Postpaid with auto-pay avoids this entirely for a trivial monthly cost (~₹200–400).

Functional requirement: the number only needs to be able to receive one SMS or voice-call OTP, during registration (Step 3). After that, it is a WhatsApp-only number.

3. What NOT to do with the new SIM

Do not insert the SIM into a phone and open WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business (the consumer apps) with this number — ever, not even briefly. If either consumer app is activated on this number, Meta enforces a mandatory 24-hour teardown wait before the number can be moved to the Business Platform. Go straight to Step 3 below with this number, skipping the consumer apps entirely.

4. Step-by-step registration procedure

Step 1 — Create the Meta Business Portfolio

  1. Go to business.facebook.com using Nagesh's (or a shared MapIt) Facebook account.
  2. Create a Business Portfolio named "MapIt" — this is the consumer-facing brand name that should appear to users.
  3. Add mapit.co.in as the business website and contact-us@mapit.co.in as the business email — both already live, which smooths every later verification step.

Step 2 — Set up MSG91 (the chosen BSP)

  1. Sign up / log in at msg91.com.
  2. Activate the WhatsApp product in the MSG91 dashboard.
  3. MSG91 provides an Embedded Signup flow that drives the entire Meta onboarding from inside their dashboard — no need to touch Meta's raw developer console directly.

Step 3 — Run Embedded Signup (creates the WABA)

  1. Inside MSG91's WhatsApp onboarding, log in with the Meta/Facebook account and select the Business Portfolio from Step 1.
  2. Enter the new phone number. Meta sends a verification code by SMS or voice call to that SIM — enter the code to confirm. (This is the one moment the number needs to function as an actual phone.)
  3. Fill in the WhatsApp Business Profile: display name (MapIt), category (e.g. "Shopping & Retail" or "Classifieds"), a short description, and optionally a business address.
  4. Completing this creates the WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) under the MapIt Business Portfolio, with MSG91 attached as the Tech Provider/BSP.

Step 4 — Display name review

The display name is reviewed separately from the phone number itself — usually 1–3 days. Keep it simple and exactly matching the business identity (MapIt) to avoid rejection or a resubmission cycle.

Step 5 — Business Verification (must be approved before Step 6)

Meta checks the legal name separately from the address / phone number. One document does not need to cover all of them.

What Meta checksWhat to use
Legal name — Nakshe VenturesPAN card + Partnership deed (both in hand) ✅
Phone number / addressA second document linking the firm's name to a phone number/address — see the options below
  1. First, fix Business Info. In Business Settings → Business Info, set the legal business name to exactly "Nakshe Ventures", matching the deed and PAN character for character. The portfolio / WhatsApp display name stays "MapIt"; that is allowed (Section 5). A legal-name mismatch is the most common rejection reason.
  2. Then go to Business Settings → Security Center → Start Verification and upload the PAN card and partnership deed for the legal name.
  3. Phone number: none of our current documents shows one. Try these in order:
    1. Nakshe Ventures' current-account bank statement: on Meta's accepted India list, and Indian bank statements usually print the registered mobile number. Make sure the number in Business Info is exactly the number on the statement.
    2. Confirm contact by email or domain instead of phone. When the upload screen asks what each document proves, tick only name (and address, if shown). Then pick email (contact-us@mapit.co.in, already working) or domain (DNS TXT record on mapit.co.in, Namecheap access needed) when asked to confirm contact.
    3. Udyam (MSME) registration: "Udyog Aadhar" is on Meta's India list, and the Udyam form collects a mobile number + email. It is free and instant at udyamregistration.gov.in using the managing partner's Aadhaar. It's not confirmed that the printed certificate shows the mobile number, so treat this as a backup. Only use the .gov.in site. Most search results are paid lookalike sites.
    4. A phone bill in the firm's name: a postpaid bill addressed to Nakshe Ventures proves both name and number, but the first bill takes about a month. Use this only if the options above fail.
  4. Review typically takes 3–7 business days. Watch the Security Center for requests for more documents.

Step 6 — Submit the OTP template for approval (only after Step 5 is approved)

In WhatsApp Manager (accessible via the MSG91 dashboard or Meta Business Suite), create a new template:

FieldValue
CategoryAuthentication
LanguageEnglish (add more later if needed)
ContentMeta's standard OTP format — verification code + expiry, optionally a "copy code" button

Authentication-category templates are low-customization and typically approve in hours to about a day.

Step 7 — Recharge and go live

Once Business Verification (Step 5), the display name (Step 4) and the template (Step 6) are all approved, add WhatsApp balance in MSG91 (Section 6) and the number is live for OTP. MSG91 exposes a send/verify API that gets wired into MapIt's backend. That integration is Session 9C's build item, tracked separately in CONTEXT.md.

5. One naming decision to lock in before Step 1

Register the Business Portfolio/WABA display name as "MapIt" (the consumer-facing brand), but set Nakshe Ventures as the legal business name in Business Info and during Business Verification (Step 5). This is the standard trade-name-vs-legal-entity pattern and avoids Meta's reviewers flagging a business-name mismatch.
