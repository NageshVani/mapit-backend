💡 Observations

OBS-01: The bigger fields also fix iPhone "zoom on tap"

iPhone Safari zooms the whole page in when you tap a text field smaller than 16px. The sign-in fields are now exactly 16px, so that zoom no longer happens. Worth noticing during the phone test.

OBS-02: "On Agreement" has no effect yet

Buyers are never shown a seller's phone number today, whatever the setting. Item 6 tidies the choice, but the actual "share my number when I agree" feature is planned for Session 9I. Testers shouldn't expect to see a phone number on any listing.

OBS-03: Link previews may show the old title for a while

WhatsApp and other apps cache link previews. A link shared before today may still show "Buy & Sell Near You" until the app refreshes its cache. Also, og-image.png is a picture and was not changed. If it has old wording printed on it, it needs a new image.
