📋 Session 09g Manual UAT: UI polish click-test

This checks the new look in real use: the Rent / Buy Property chips, real prices on every pin, the location and distance dropdowns in the header, the single Post button, the foldable side panel and the tidier listing panel. Claude's automated pass is in session-09g-uat-report.html (44 pass, 0 fail, 2 warnings, 1 left for you).

Test on: https://mapit-backend-git-uat-nagesh-n-arun.vercel.app. Use a laptop/desktop and your phone. Section 5 is laptop only.

You'll need: your normal account, with at least one listing of your own and ideally two saved locations.

⚠️ Don't use uat.mapit.co.in: it still shows the live site, without these changes. The first load can take 5–10 seconds while the preview wakes up; that's normal. Nothing in this checklist needs you to post or delete anything.

1Category chips

1a
The chips read All · Rent Property · Buy Property · Vehicles · Household
  1. Open the uat link and sign in (wait up to 10 seconds on the first load)
  2. Look at the row of category buttons at the top of the side panel (on a phone, under the header)
  3. Expected: You see, in this order: All · 🔑 Rent Property · 🏠 Buy Property · 🚗 Vehicles · 🏡 Household
  4. Expected: All is highlighted when the app first opens
1b
Rent Property shows only rentals
  1. Tap 🔑 Rent Property
  2. Look at the pins on the map and the cards in the list
  3. Expected: Only rental homes appear. Their prices end in /mo (e.g. ₹28K/mo)
1c
Buy Property shows only homes for sale
  1. Tap 🏠 Buy Property
  2. Expected: Only properties for sale appear, with no /mo on the prices
  3. Expected: Tap All again and everything comes back
1d
Chips fit on a small phone
  1. On your phone, look at the same chip row
  2. Expected: Every chip is fully readable. On a narrow phone, the longer labels may wrap onto two neat lines, which is fine. Nothing is cut off, and you don't have to scroll sideways to read them

2Prices

2a
Pins and cards always show a real price
  1. Tap All and look over the map pins and the list cards
  2. Expected: Every price is a real amount like ₹26K, ₹1.3L or ₹1.25Cr
  3. Expected: No pin or card shows the words "negotiable" or "per month" in place of the price
  4. Expected: A listing with no price says Price on request
2b
"Negotiable" now appears inside the listing details
  1. Open a listing you know was posted as negotiable (several of the sample listings were)
  2. Expected: "Negotiable" appears as a small note inside the details, not as the price
💡 If you can't find one, mark Pass with a note "none found".
2c
Big prices aren't cut off
  1. Open a listing priced in crores (e.g. ₹1.25Cr) and a rental (e.g. ₹28K/mo)
  2. Expected: The full price is visible in the listing panel, not cut off like "₹1.25…" or "₹25K/…"

3Header: location and distance

3a
The location button shows where you are searching
  1. Look at the top header, next to Explore
  2. Expected: It reads 📍 My location · ‹your active location› ▾. On a phone it may be shortened to 📍 ‹location› ▾
3b
Switch between saved locations
  1. Tap the 📍 location button
  2. A small menu opens. Pick a different saved location
  3. Expected: The menu shows ✓ next to the current location, plus ＋ Add or manage locations… at the bottom
  4. Expected: Picking another location moves the map there straight away, without an "Are you sure?" popup
💡 Need a second saved location? Use "＋ Add or manage locations…" to add one first.
3c
"Add or manage locations…" opens My Locations
  1. Open the 📍 menu again and tap ＋ Add or manage locations…
  2. Expected: The usual My Locations window opens
3d
Explore and back
  1. Tap 🗺 Explore, then drag the map somewhere else
  2. Now tap the 📍 location button once
  3. Expected: In Explore mode, listings follow wherever you move the map
  4. Expected: Tapping 📍 brings you back to your saved location
3e
Distance dropdown
  1. Tap Within 5 km ▾
  2. Pick 10 km, then try Whole city
  3. Expected: The menu offers 1 km, 3 km, 5 km, 10 km, Region (50 km) and Whole city
  4. Expected: The button label updates, and more (or fewer) pins appear on the map

4Posting and naming

4a
One Post button, and the form starts on Rent
  1. Look at the header: there should be a single orange ＋ Post a Listing button and no "Browse" button
  2. Tap it, choose Real Estate if it isn't already selected
  3. Expected: Only one Post button exists
  4. Expected: In the form, Rent is already selected for Real Estate
💡 Just close the form afterwards; you don't need to post anything.
4b
"My Ads" is now "My Listings" everywhere
  1. Check the third tab in the side panel
  2. Open your avatar menu → ❓ Help, then 📖 User Guide
  3. Expected: The tab says 📋 My Listings
  4. Expected: Help and the User Guide also say "My Listings". "My Ads" appears nowhere

5Collapsible side panel (laptop/desktop only)

5a
Fold the panel into a slim icon strip
  1. On a laptop or desktop, find the small ‹ tab on the left edge of the map and click it
  2. Expected: The side panel shrinks to a narrow strip of icons, and the map gets wider
  3. Expected: Hovering over each icon shows its name (e.g. Rent Property, Saved)
5b
Clicking a pin opens the panel, closing it folds it back
  1. With the panel folded, click any pin on the map
  2. Then close the listing (✕)
  3. Expected: The panel opens to show that listing
  4. Expected: After closing, it folds back into the slim strip by itself
5c
Tab icons open the panel and keep it open
  1. With the panel folded, click the ❤ (Saved) or 📋 (My Listings) icon in the strip
  2. Expected: The panel opens on that tab and stays open
5d
The app remembers your choice
  1. Fold the panel, then reload the page (Cmd+R / Ctrl+R)
  2. Expected: After reloading, the panel is still folded. Click › to open it again

6Listing detail panel

6a
The full title shows, and the buttons have their own row
  1. Open a listing with a long title (e.g. "2BHK Semi-Furnished Apartment for Rent — Koramangala")
  2. Expected: The title shows on up to two lines, not cut short like "Tata T…"
  3. Expected: Below the title is a row with a solid green 🤝 I'm Interested (or 💬 Chat with seller) button, plus ♡ 🚩 🔗
6b
The interest note opens right below
  1. On someone else's listing, tap 🤝 I'm Interested
  2. Expected: The box for your note opens directly below the button row
💡 No need to send it. Close or cancel.
6c
Your own listing shows Edit
  1. Open one of your own listings (from My Listings)
  2. Expected: The main button is a solid blue ✏️ Edit Listing

7Map and phone layout

7a
The map credit is shorter
  1. Look at the bottom-right corner of the map
  2. Expected: It reads © Mapbox © OpenStreetMap. The "🇺🇦 Leaflet |" part is gone
7b
The phone layout feels right
  1. On your phone, use the app normally for a minute: browse, open a listing, use both dropdowns
  2. Expected: The header fits in two tidy rows with nothing overlapping
  3. Expected: You never need to scroll sideways
  4. Expected: Buttons are easy to tap

8Quick regression

8a
Everyday things still work
  1. Sign out and sign in again
  2. Save a listing with ♡
  3. Open the Inbox from your avatar menu
  4. As admin, open the /admin page
  5. Expected: All of these work as they did before 9G
