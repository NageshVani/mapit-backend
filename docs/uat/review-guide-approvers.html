How to review listings on MapIt

A short guide for the MapIt review team. Before a new listing appears on the MapIt map, one of us checks it. Most listings take less than a minute. You only need your phone.

1Get started (only once)

  1. The MapIt team sends you a personal invite link on WhatsApp. Open it on your phone. It works once and is valid for 7 days.
  2. You see Create account with a green "You have been invited" note. Fill in your email, your name and a password (8+ characters, one capital letter, one symbol), then tap Email me a code.
  3. Open your email, find the 6-digit code (check Spam if it doesn't arrive in a minute), type it in and tap Request access.
  4. You see "Thanks": the MapIt team now activates your account, usually the same day. Tap Check again later, and the listings appear.
💡 Next time, open mapit.co.in/review and sign in with your email and password. Add it to your home screen: in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen; on iPhone tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
⚠️ Already use MapIt with the same email to buy or sell? Creating your review account sets a new password for that MapIt account too. Use the new password in both places.

If the link says "not valid, already used or expired", ask the MapIt team for a new one.

2Look at the listing

You see one listing at a time, oldest first. Nobody else on the team gets the same listing while you have it open.

What you see, top to bottom: the photos (swipe sideways, tap one to see it big), the title and price, the category and area, the seller's first name and how long the listing has waited, the description, and the details.

Check these things

  • The photos show the item that is being sold, in the right category.
  • The category is right (a scooter is under 2 Wheelers, not Cars).
  • The price is filled in and looks realistic.
  • The area makes sense for the listing.
  • There is no phone number, email or web link in the title or description. Buyers contact sellers through MapIt chat.
  • The description is honest and complete enough for a buyer.
🚩 A red "Flagged" note means our automatic check noticed something, like a phone number in the text. Look extra carefully, but you decide: the flag is a hint, not a verdict.

(These screenshots use test listings, so some photos don't match the title. On a real listing, that is exactly when you would tap Change.)

3Your 4 choices

The buttons are at the bottom of the screen. They stay grey for a moment when a new listing appears, so a quick double tap can never decide the next one by mistake.

✓ Approve: everything looks fine. The listing goes live on the map straight away, and the seller gets an email that it's live.
✏️ Change: something small needs fixing (wrong photo, phone number in the text, missing price…). Pick a reason, add a short note if it helps, and tap Send back. The seller fixes it and sends it again; it comes back to the queue.
✕ Reject: the listing can't be on MapIt (not allowed, a duplicate, spam or fake). Pick a reason and tap Reject.
Skip for now: not sure? Skip it. It goes back to the queue for you or someone else later. Nothing is sent to the seller.

"The seller will see"

Before you send, the grey box shows exactly what the seller reads in the email and in their My Listings. Write the note kindly, as if you were helping a neighbour. Your name is never shown to the seller.

5Good to know

  • Who am I signed in as? The orange line under the header says "Signed in as …". Check it if the phone is shared.
  • "about 12 left" at the top is how many listings are waiting for the whole team, not only for you. There is no rush.
  • If you leave a listing open for more than 5 minutes, it may go back to the queue for someone else. That's fine: just continue.
  • "This listing just changed or was already reviewed" is normal. The seller edited it, or someone else decided first. The next listing loads by itself.
  • 🎉 All clear means nothing is waiting. 👍 Nothing more for now means only the ones you skipped are left: tap Check again to see them.
  • You never see your own listings in the queue. Someone else reviews those.
  • You only see the seller's first name and the area, never their phone, email or exact location. Please keep anything you see private.
  • On a shared phone, tap Sign out at the top when you finish.

👥 For Nagesh & Arun: the Team panel

Only admins see the ☰ menu at the top right. Tap ☰ → Team.

  1. Invite someone: type their name and tap Create invite link. Tap Share on WhatsApp (or Copy link). The link is shown only once; if it gets lost, cancel it and create a new one.
  2. When they have signed up, they appear under Waiting. Tap Activate (or Ignore if you don't know them).
  3. Revoke under Active turns access off on their next tap. Activate again under Revoked brings them back.
  4. The Invites list shows each link as open, used (and by whom), expired or cancelled. Cancel an open link you no longer need.

The admin page's Pending Listings tile now opens this review queue.
